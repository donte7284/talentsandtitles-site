Seven good years come to Egypt, and the earth brings forth by handfuls. Joseph lays up the food in the cities. He gathers corn as the sand of the sea, until he stops counting. Then the good years end, and the famine comes over all the land. The people cry to Pharaoh for bread. Pharaoh says, "Go unto Joseph." Joseph opens the storehouses. <break time="0.8s" /> The famine doesn't make the plan. It finds out who had one. What are you storing up while the years are good?
