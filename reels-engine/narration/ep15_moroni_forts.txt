Moroni builds walls before the enemy ever arrives. He raises small forts, throws up banks of earth, and builds walls of stone round about the cities. <break time="0.8s" /> In the weakest fortifications he places the greater number of men. <break time="0.8s" /> Then the Lamanites come, and find a ridge of earth so high their stones and arrows cannot take effect. The chief captains are astonished exceedingly at the wisdom of their preparing. Prepare where you are weakest. Where is your weakest wall? Send this to someone who has not fortified yet.
