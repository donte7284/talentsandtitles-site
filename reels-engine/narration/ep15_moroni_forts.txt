Captain Moroni builds walls before the enemy arrives. He throws up banks of earth, and builds walls of stone round about the cities. In the weakest fortifications he places the greater number of men. <break time="0.8s" /> Then the Lamanites come toward Ammonihah. But the Nephites have dug up a ridge of earth so high that the stones and arrows cannot take effect. <break time="0.8s" /> The chief captains are astonished exceedingly, because of the wisdom of the Nephites in preparing their places of security. Where are your weakest walls? Send this to someone who prepares in peacetime.
