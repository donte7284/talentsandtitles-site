Nephi's bow breaks. It was fine steel, and without it, the family gets no food. His brothers are angry. Their own bows have lost their spring. <break time="0.8s" /> Nephi makes a new bow out of wood, and an arrow from a straight stick. Then he asks his father: where should I go to find food? <break time="0.8s" /> The Lord answers, Nephi climbs the mountain it points to, and he brings home food. He made it from what he had. Then he asked. What do you have in your hands? Send this to someone whose tool just broke.
