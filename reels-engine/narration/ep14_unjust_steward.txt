A steward about to lose his job plans ahead, and his lord commends him for it. He is told: give an account of thy stewardship. He says within himself: I cannot dig; to beg I am ashamed. <break time="0.8s" /> So he calls his lord's debtors. A hundred measures of oil: write fifty. A hundred measures of wheat: write fourscore. <break time="0.8s" /> The lord commended him, because he had done wisely. Copy the foresight, not the fraud.
