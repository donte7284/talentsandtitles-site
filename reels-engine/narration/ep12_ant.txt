An ant gets the work done with no boss. Solomon says: go to the ant, thou sluggard, consider her ways, and be wise. She has no guide, overseer, or ruler. <break time="0.8s" /> Yet she provides her meat in the summer, and gathers her food in the harvest. Nobody checks her work. She does it while the season is open. <break time="0.8s" /> Start before anyone makes you. What are you waiting to be told to do? Send this to someone who needs a boss to begin.
