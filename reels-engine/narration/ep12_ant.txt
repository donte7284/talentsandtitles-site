Proverbs sends the lazy man to an ant to learn. Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise. <break time="0.8s" /> She has no guide. No overseer. No ruler. Nobody stands over her. <break time="0.8s" /> Yet she provides her meat in the summer, and gathers her food in the harvest. She gathers it in, and no one makes her do it. Nobody is coming to make you. Gather in the summer. What are you gathering while nobody's watching? Send this to someone who works without a boss.
