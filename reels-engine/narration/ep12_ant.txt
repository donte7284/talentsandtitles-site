Proverbs sends the lazy man to school with an ant. "Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise." <break time="0.8s" /> Look at what she doesn't have. No guide. No overseer. No ruler. Nobody stands over her, and nobody tells her what to do. <break time="0.8s" /> Still, she provides her meat in the summer, and gathers her food in the harvest, before anyone makes her. Nobody is coming to make you start. What would you build this week if no one was watching? Send this to someone who just became their own boss.
