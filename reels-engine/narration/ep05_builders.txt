Two men build a house. One builds on a rock. One builds on sand. Then the rain descends, the floods come, and the winds blow and beat on both houses. The house on the rock does not fall. The house on the sand falls, and great is the fall of it. Jesus says the wise man hears his words and does them. <break time="0.8s" /> Same storm. Same weather. The difference was the foundation.
