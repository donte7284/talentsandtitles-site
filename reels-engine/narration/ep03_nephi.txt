Nephi is told to build a ship. He's never built one. He has no shipyard, no crew, and no tools. Listen to his first question. He doesn't ask how. He doesn't ask why me. He asks, "Where do I find ore so I can make the tools?" His brothers call him a fool, but he's already making the tools. He started one step before the beginning. You don't need the ship yet. You need the ore. What's the tool you have to make first? Send this to someone who feels unqualified.
