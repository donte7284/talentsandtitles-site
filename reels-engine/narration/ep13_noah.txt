Noah builds an ark before the flood comes. God tells him: make thee an ark of gopher wood, and pitch it within and without. Three hundred cubits long, fifty wide, thirty high. <break time="0.8s" /> A window above. A door in the side. Lower, second, and third stories. And food, gathered to keep them alive. <break time="0.8s" /> Thus did Noah. According to all that God commanded him, so did he. He built it first. What are you building before you need it? Send this to someone waiting for the storm.
