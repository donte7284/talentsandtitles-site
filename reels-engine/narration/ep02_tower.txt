A man starts building a tower. He lays the foundation, then the money runs out. Now everyone who walks past sees it, a foundation with nothing on it. They laugh. He started and couldn't finish. Jesus asks, "Which of you wouldn't sit down first and count the cost?" Starting is cheap. Everybody starts. The count is what separates a tower from a punchline. What have you started without doing the math? Send this to someone about to start something big.
