Alma hands his son a set of old records, and says it may look like foolishness. My son Helaman, take the records. Keep them, and hand them down from one generation to another. <break time="0.8s" /> Ye may suppose that this is foolishness in me. <break time="0.8s" /> But by small and simple things are great things brought to pass. These records enlarged the memory of this people. Small means. Great things. Keep doing the small thing. What small thing are you about to quit because it looks like nothing? Send this to someone doing the small things every day.
