Three servants are trusted with their lord's money, and the one who plays it safe loses it. To one, five talents. To another, two. To another, one. <break time="0.8s" /> Five trades, and makes other five. Two gains other two. One digs in the earth, and hides it. <break time="0.8s" /> After a long time, the lord comes to reckon. Well done, good and faithful servant. The one: I was afraid. Take the talent from him. He kept it safe, and still lost it. What are you burying because you're afraid to lose it? Send this to someone sitting on a gift.
