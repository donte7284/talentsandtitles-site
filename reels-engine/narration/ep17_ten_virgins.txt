Ten wait with lamps for the bridegroom, and five get shut out. Five are wise. Five are foolish. The foolish take no oil with them. <break time="0.8s" /> The bridegroom tarries, and they all sleep. At midnight, a cry: Behold, the bridegroom cometh. <break time="0.8s" /> Give us of your oil; our lamps are gone out. Not so. Go and buy for yourselves. While they go, the bridegroom comes, and the door is shut. You can't borrow ready at midnight. What are you waiting to prepare until you need it? Send this to someone who will get ready later.
