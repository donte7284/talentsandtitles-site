A widow owes a debt she can't pay. The creditor is coming for her two sons. The prophet asks her: what do you have in your house? "Nothing, just one jar of oil." He says, "Borrow every empty jar your neighbors own, not a few. Then shut the door and pour." She pours. Every jar fills, and the oil only stops when she runs out of jars. The supply was never the limit. The jars were. What do you already have in your house? Send this to someone who thinks they're starting with nothing.
