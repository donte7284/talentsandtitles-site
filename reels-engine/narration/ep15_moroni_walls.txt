An army comes for an easy target, and finds walls. Captain Moroni has been preparing: small forts, banks of earth, walls of stone round about their cities. In the weakest places, he puts the most men. <break time="0.8s" /> The Lamanites march on Ammonihah. They destroyed it once; they expect easy prey. But the Nephites have dug a ridge of earth so high, their stones and arrows cannot take effect. <break time="0.8s" /> The chief captains are astonished exceedingly. Build the wall before the army comes. Where is your weakest wall? Send this to someone bracing for a hard season.
