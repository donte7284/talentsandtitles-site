Nephi breaks his bow, the one made of fine steel. His brothers are angry. Their own bows have lost their springs. Everyone is murmuring. Nephi makes a bow out of wood, and an arrow out of a straight stick. Then he asks his father, "Whither shall I go to obtain food?"
