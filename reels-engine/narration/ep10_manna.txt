The bread they saved for tomorrow rots overnight. Morning in the wilderness: something small and round lies on the ground, fine as frost. Moses says: this is the bread the Lord has given you. <break time="0.8s" /> Gather an omer for every person. Some gather more, some less, and nobody has too much or too little. Then Moses says: leave none of it till the morning. Some do anyway. <break time="0.8s" /> It breeds worms, and it stinks. Enough for today. What are you holding past its day? Send this to someone who is afraid to let go.
