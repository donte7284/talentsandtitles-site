Ruth says to Naomi: "Let me now go to the field, and glean." She goes, and gleans after the reapers, in the field of a man named Boaz. <break time="0.8s" /> She keeps at it from the morning until now. Boaz sees her, and tells his young men: let some handfuls fall on purpose, and leave them for her. <break time="0.8s" /> She gleans until evening, and it is about an ephah of barley.
