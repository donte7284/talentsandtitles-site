Jesus compares the kingdom of heaven to the least of all seeds. A grain of mustard seed. A man takes it, and sows it in his field. <break time="0.8s" /> It is the least of all seeds. <break time="0.8s" /> But when it is grown, it is the greatest among herbs, and becometh a tree. And the birds of the air come and lodge in the branches. Big things start too small to notice. Plant it anyway. What are you not planting because it looks too small?
