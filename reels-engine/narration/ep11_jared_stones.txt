He needs light for eight boats, so he makes sixteen stones. The brother of Jared goes up the mount, and melts sixteen small stones out of a rock, white and clear, like glass. <break time="0.8s" /> He carries them to the top and prays: Lord, touch these stones, that they may shine forth in darkness. <break time="0.8s" /> The Lord stretches forth his hand and touches the stones one by one. He did the work he could. Then he asked for the rest. What can you make before you ask? Send this to someone waiting for light.
