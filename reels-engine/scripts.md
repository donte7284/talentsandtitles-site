# Parables for Builders: scripts

The voice recording is the source of truth for wording. On-screen reference for No. 3 is 1 NEPHI 17 : 8–18 (the "fool" line is 17:17).
**No. 1 — The widow's oil (2 Kings 4:1-7)**
A widow owes a debt she can't pay. The creditor is coming for her two sons. The prophet asks one question: what do you have in your house? Nothing. Just one jar of oil. He says: borrow every empty jar your neighbours own. Not a few. Then shut the door and pour. She pours. Every jar fills. And the oil only stops when she runs out of jars. The supply was never the limit. The jars were. What do you already have in your house? Send this to someone who thinks they're starting with nothing.

**No. 2 — The unfinished tower (Luke 14:28-30)**
A man starts building a tower. He lays the foundation. Then the money runs out. Now everyone who walks past sees it. A foundation, with nothing on it. And they laugh: he started, and couldn't finish. Jesus asks: which of you wouldn't sit down first and count the cost? Starting is cheap. Everybody starts. The count is what separates a tower from a punchline. What have you started without doing the math? Send this to someone about to start something big.

**No. 3 — Nephi's ship (1 Nephi 17:8-18)**
Nephi is told to build a ship. He's never built one. He has no shipyard, no crew, and no tools. His brothers call him a fool. But listen to his first question. He doesn't ask how. He doesn't ask why me. He asks: where do I find ore, so I can make the tools? He started one step before the beginning. You don't need the ship yet. You need the ore. What's the tool you have to make first? Send this to someone who feels unqualified.
